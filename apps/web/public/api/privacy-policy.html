Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lumix Social Manager collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal data of its users, in compliance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law No. 13,709/2018), the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other applicable legislation.

1. Data Controller

Lumix Social Manager is operated by Lumix Agency ("we", "our", or "Company"), which is the data controller for personal data collected through the Service.

Lumix Social Manager — operated by Lumix Agency
Email: hercules@lumixagencia.com
Website: https://lumixagencia.com

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data provided by you

  • Full name and email address when creating your Lumix Social Manager account;
  • Information about the company or organization you represent;
  • Content (videos, photos, text, and metadata) that you submit for publishing through Lumix Social Manager.

2.2 Data obtained through third-party integrations

When you connect your social media accounts (such as TikTok) to Lumix Social Manager, we receive the following data through official APIs:

  • TikTok: User ID (open_id), display name, avatar, creator privacy settings (privacy_level_options), publishing capabilities, and OAuth access tokens.
Lumix Social Manager only accesses data strictly necessary for providing the Service, in accordance with the scopes authorized by the user: user.info.basic, video.publish, and video.upload.

2.3 Data collected automatically

  • IP address and browser information;
  • Usage data and Lumix Social Manager access logs;
  • Essential cookies for platform functionality.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Lumix Social Manager uses your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery: authenticating your identity, connecting your social media accounts, and publishing content on your behalf through Lumix Social Manager;
  • Communication: sending notifications about publication status, Lumix Social Manager updates, and support;
  • Security: protecting against fraud, unauthorized access, and abuse;
  • Legal compliance: fulfilling applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Legal Basis

We process personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: when you authorize the connection of your social media accounts to Lumix Social Manager through the OAuth flow;
  • Contract performance: for the provision of the Lumix Social Manager service;
  • Legitimate interest: to improve the security and quality of Lumix Social Manager.

5. Data Sharing

Your personal data collected by Lumix Social Manager may be shared with:

  • Social media platforms: when you use Lumix Social Manager to publish content (e.g., TikTok receives your content and associated metadata);
  • Infrastructure providers: hosting and storage services necessary to operate Lumix Social Manager;
  • Competent authorities: when required by law or court order.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Storage and Security

Lumix Social Manager stores your data on secure servers with the following protection measures:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest;
  • Role-based access control (RBAC);
  • OAuth access tokens stored in encrypted form;
  • Access monitoring and auditing;
  • Regular backups with data loss protection.

7. Data Retention

Your personal data is retained by Lumix Social Manager only for as long as necessary:

  • Account data: retained while your Lumix Social Manager account is active and for up to 30 days after closure;
  • Published content: processed and transmitted to the destination platform; Lumix Social Manager does not retain permanent copies;
  • Access tokens: stored while the integration is active; immediately revoked upon disconnection from Lumix Social Manager;
  • Access logs: retained for up to 6 months for security and compliance purposes.

8. Your Rights

In accordance with applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal data processed by Lumix Social Manager:

  • Right to access your personal data;
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Right to erasure (deletion) of your data;
  • Right to data portability;
  • Right to restrict or object to data processing;
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time;
  • Right to information about third parties with whom your data has been shared.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hercules@lumixagencia.com. We will respond within 15 business days.

9. Revoking Access

You may revoke Lumix Social Manager's access to your social media accounts at any time:

  • Through your account settings on Lumix Social Manager;
  • Directly through the security settings of the social media platform (e.g., TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions);
  • By contacting us to request disconnection and token deletion.

10. Cookies

Lumix Social Manager uses strictly necessary cookies for the operation of the Service. We do not use advertising tracking cookies.

11. International Data Transfers

When using integrations with third-party platforms such as TikTok through Lumix Social Manager, your data may be transferred to servers located outside of Brazil. These transfers are carried out in accordance with the official APIs of the platforms and are subject to their respective privacy policies.

12. Children's Privacy

Lumix Social Manager is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy for Lumix Social Manager periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes by email or through the Service.
