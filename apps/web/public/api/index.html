How it works

1

Connect your TikTok account

Click "Connect with TikTok" and authorize Lumix Social Manager via TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 flow. Your credentials are never stored — we use secure access tokens.

2

Upload your content

Upload videos or photos, write your caption, set hashtags, choose privacy level, and configure engagement settings (comments, duet, stitch).

3

Preview and publish

Review everything before posting. Publish directly to TikTok or send as a draft to the creator's inbox for final review. Track the publish status in real time.
